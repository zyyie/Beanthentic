Frequently Asked Questions

Go to Account Security and fill out the password form. Enter your current password, then a new password (minimum 8 characters).

Open Activity Log under Settings. Use the search bar to filter by date, action, details, or IP address.

Upload Kapeng Barako GI documents in the IPOPHL module. Analysis checks product identity and filing themes (origin, history, process, control). Ready / Not Ready is advisory — it does not file with IPOPHL.

Records freeze when the farmer chose Sell or Drop-off & Sell and their price application is still pending, or when the account is inactive. Enable self-sale or approve the price application to unlock.

Open Client Report under Client Transaction. Reports are submitted from Client Web. Use status filters and the linked transaction when evidence is attached.

Use the bell icon in the dashboard header (not the sidebar). Open it to review live alerts, mark items read, or dismiss them.
